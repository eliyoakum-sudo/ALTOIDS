hotel spaces:
this place is located *behind* the singularity sun of the ascended 

realm, just out of veiw for anyone trying to look, this place is an 
infinitly spanning hallway, regular hotel carpet and walls, each 
door is spaced 10 feet apart each rooom is labled "1A" through
"1Z" and then "2A" to "2Z" and so on infinitly going.... on... and on... and on...

no one had stayed in that one room before, the sense comes from

cleanliness of the rooms

how many times the door is opened and walked

the neighborhood:
a regular suburban neigborhood... but no one's in the houses,
they're fully furnished, its mid day not a hint of orange in the sky
the tvs, microwaves, ovens, and electronics work, there is food in
the fridges and pantries, theres not a car in any driveway even 
showing that there *could* be people in the neighborhood
no matter how far you go outside of the neighborhood,
when you turn beyond a treeline, look up to the sky then
back down,or even just closing your eyes and opening 
them again after a few seconds you appear back in the neighborhood... 

you can see the neighborhood faintly while wandering the acended realm
no matter how hard you try you cant get there


closed road:
when someone wanders to far outside the neighborhood without being 
returned they will stumble upon a foggy, closed road, the fog is so thick that
the fear of what could be in the fog is so powerful, that, no matter
how hard someone may try, they cant convince themself to go off
the road, every 100 feet down the road there is a "road closed for construction" sign,
despite the road beind closed there are no cracks, potholes, or even any sounds
of construction,because there is fog, someone would suspect the air would be damp,
but no, the air is completely dry, it feels impossible, both the fact that the air
isnt damp, the fact that there isnt the faintest sound of construction in the air,
and the fact that there isnt anything wrong with the road, feels impossible


static ocean:
an unmeasurable distance from where soemone would start in the ascended realm
there is an ocean, this ocean isnt an ocean, its a sea of tv static, the more someone
looks into the static the more they just want to step in, the effects of some one stepping
into this sea of static is... nothing, even though there are no actual effects, anyone who 
happens to step in will feel a sense of extreem dread and misery, this feeling wont
shake off until someone who was in the static ocean, came out of the static ocean,
but, the dread and misery would paralyze them, making them unable to leave the "water"
